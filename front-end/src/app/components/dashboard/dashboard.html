Learn more about CRM

A CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.It is a system, software, or strategy used by businesses to manage interactions with customers and potential customers in one central place. A CRM helps a business .Store customer information.Track conversations and interactions.Manage sales leads and deals.Improve customer service.Build long-term customer relationships.A Customer Relationship Management (CRM) website serves as a digital hub for managing all company interactions with current and potential customers. It is designed to centralize, streamline, and improve customer relationships, ultimately boosting sales, efficiency, and customer satisfaction. A CRM system is a data-driven, often cloud-based, software solution that stores, tracks, and manages all customer-related information—such as contact details, purchase history, and support tickets—in one centralized location. It acts as a single source of truth for sales, marketing, and customer service teams, ensuring everyone is on the same page.
